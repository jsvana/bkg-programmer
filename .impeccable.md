## Design Context

### Users

Amateur radio operators (technical hobbyists) configuring Quansheng UV-K5 / UV-K1
handhelds that run F4HWN-family custom firmware. They are technically literate:
they read firmware source, talk in hex offsets and CRCs, and care that the tool
admits what it knows vs. what it doesn't. They are usually at a desk with the
radio tethered by USB-C and a service manual open in another tab.

### Brand Personality

**Precise. Honest. Technical.**

Workshop-tool feel — closer to an oscilloscope front panel or a Hammond-enclosure
test fixture than a SaaS dashboard. Cites firmware file:line citations without
apology. Surfaces uncertainty (e.g., "this radio is AMBIGUOUS uv-k1/uv-k5-v3")
rather than papering over it. No marketing copy, no aspirational language.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Tone**: Service-manual utilitarian, with measurement-equipment cues. Think
  small all-caps labels in monospace next to plain-prose explanations. Status
  lights, address readouts, version strings — surfaced as data, not decoration.
- **Hierarchy**: Three distinct top-level tools. From a hub, the operator picks
  one and the workspace focuses on it. Connection state is persistent across all
  tools (a status rail, not a panel).
- **Theme**: System-driven (light + dark). Operators work in both garages and
  daylit offices.
- **Anti-references**: SaaS-style stat cards, gradient hero text, glassmorphism,
  decorative icon-above-heading layouts, "AI-cyan" accents.

### Design Principles

1. **One tool at a time.** The page never shows more than one of {Flash, Program,
   Splash} at full fidelity. The hub uses prominence to guide; once chosen, the
   workspace commits.
2. **Status is structural.** Port name, identified radio, firmware version, and
   lockscreen flag belong in a persistent rail — not buried in a panel.
3. **Cite, don't reassure.** When the firmware behavior is empirically unknown,
   say so on screen. Use monospace for anything that came from the radio.
4. **Disciplined neutrals, single accent.** Warm-tinted grays (toward an amber
   signal hue), one accent color used sparingly. No gradients.
5. **No nested cards.** Flat sections separated by hairline rules. Visual rhythm
   comes from spacing and type, not from boxes inside boxes.
